# Playground

Games and toys to enjoy with everyone who has the mod, on any server: **Tag**, **Hide and Seek**, a **swing set** and **bowling**, with more on the way.

*This mod was created with the assistance of AI.*

## Tag

1. Click the little **star button** on the left of your screen (or press **J**) and choose **Start a round of Tag**. Drag the button to put it wherever you like.
2. Everyone nearby with Playground gets a notice and has **10 seconds** to join by pressing **Y**.
3. Everyone who joined is sent back to spawn and a **5 second countdown** begins.
4. One player is picked at random to be **it**. A glowing white star floats above their head, and they run **30% faster**. "It" has to wait out the countdown, so everyone else gets a head start.
5. Touch someone to make them it. You can't tag back the player who just tagged you for 3 seconds.
6. After **3 minutes** the round ends and everyone sees how long each player was it. **Least time wins.**

You can leave a round any time from the J menu. If whoever is it leaves, someone else is picked.

## Hide and Seek

1. Open the Playground menu (the star button, or **J**). Tick or untick **Found hiders join the seekers**, then choose **Start Hide and Seek**.
2. Everyone with Playground has **10 seconds** to join with **Y**. Everyone who joins is sent back to spawn.
3. One player, picked at random, is the **seeker**. Their screen goes dark and they can't move for **45 seconds** while everyone else hides.
4. Then the search is on, for **5 minutes**. When a seeker gets close to a hider, **F  Found!** appears over them. Press **F** to find them.
5. Found hiders either **join the seekers** and help hunt, or are **out** and watch. Whoever started the round picks which.
6. Seekers can't see the hiders' name tags, so names don't give anyone away.
7. Find everyone and the **seekers win**. If anyone is still hidden when time runs out, the **hiders win**. Everyone then sees who was found, when, and by whom.

## Swing set

1. Open the Playground menu and choose **Place a swing set**. It goes up right in front of you, facing you. Choose **Pick up your swing set** to take it down. Everyone can put up one.
2. Walk up to a seat and press **E** to sit. There are two seats, so bring a friend.
3. The swing sways a little on its own. Press **Space right at the top of each swing**, front or back, to pump and go higher. A bar at the bottom of the screen lights up when it's time. A press at the wrong moment costs a little height.
4. At full height, press **Space at the top of the forward swing** to let go and fly across the sky, just like a blue mushroom.
5. Press **E** to get off.

Players with Playground see the swing set. Players without it still see you sitting and swinging, just in mid air.

## Bowling

1. Open the Playground menu and choose **Place a bowling lane**. A lane runs away from you with ten pins at the far end. Choose **Pick up your bowling lane** to take it down. The ground needs to be fairly flat.
2. Walk onto the approach (the floor in front of the lane) and press **E** to bowl. One person bowls on a lane at a time.
3. **A** and **D** slide you across the lane. **Q** and **R** turn your aim. The **left** and **right arrow keys** put spin on the ball: it runs straight over the oily first stretch, then hooks toward the side you spun. A dotted blue trail shows where the ball will go.
4. Press **Space** to start the power bar, and **Space** again to roll. The fuller the bar, the faster the ball.
5. Pins knock into each other. Ten frames, two balls each, strikes and spares count extra, and the tenth frame gives bonus balls. A score board for the lane shows for everyone nearby.
6. After the last ball press **Space** for a new game, or **E** to leave the lane.

Everyone with Playground sees the same pins fall. Players without it don't see the lane.

## Good to know

- Only players with Playground take part or see the star. Everyone else sees nothing unusual.
- Everything goes through the Steam lobby, so it works on any server and needs nothing from the host.
